Vegetable Korma

A gently spiced vegetable curry with a creamy, aromatic sauce.

Category Main
Yield 3–4 servings
Time About 40 min

Ingredients

Vegetables

  • 2–3 cups mixed vegetables, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped

Korma base

  • 1 tbsp coconut oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tsp mustard seeds, 2 bay leaves, 1 cinnamon stick, 1 star anise
  • 1 tsp garlic-ginger paste
  • 1 tsp salt, or to taste
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp fennel powder
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground cardamon
  • a pinch of nutmeg and ground cloves
  • 1 tbsp cashew or almond butter
  • 1/2 cup Greek yoghurt
  • Salt, to taste

Finish

  • Fresh coriander
  • A squeeze of lemon, if needed

Method

  1. Heat the oil, add cumin seeds, bay leaves, cinnamon stick and star anise, then cook the onion until soft. Add garlic-ginger paste and cook briefly.
  2. Add the tomato and powdered spices and cook until the mixture becomes thick and aromatic.
  3. Add the vegetables with a small splash of water. Cover and cook until nearly tender.
  4. Stir in the nut butter, and simmer gently until the vegetables are cooked and the sauce is cohesive, adjust water, take 1/2 cup of liquid and mix with Greek yoghurt. Do not boil anymore (otherwise the yoghurt will separate). Adjust salt and acidity.
  5. Finish with fresh coriander.

Serving

Serve with rice, roti, or naan.

Notes

A flexible korma entry: the vegetable mix can change with what is available. The final sauce should stay creamy and aromatic rather than aggressively hot.
