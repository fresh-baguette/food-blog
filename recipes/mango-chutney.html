Indian Sweet Mango Chutney

Ripe mango cooked down with nigella, ginger-garlic, green chilli and warm spices — sweet, sharp and still a little chunky.

CategoryCondiment
Yield1 small jar
TimeAbout 20 min

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe mango, cubed
  • 1/2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 green chilli, finely chopped
  • 1/2 tsp nigella seeds
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp ground coriander
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 tbsp vinegar

Method

  1. Heat the oil and fry the ginger-garlic paste, green chilli and nigella seeds for about 30 seconds.
  2. Add the mango, turmeric, coriander, cumin, salt and sugar.
  3. Cook gently until the sugar has melted and the mango begins to soften.
  4. Add the vinegar and continue cooking until smooth and jammy while retaining some mango pieces.

Notes

Keep some mango intact: the best texture is a glossy jam-like base with soft fruit pieces running through it.
